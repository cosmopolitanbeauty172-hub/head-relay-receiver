# Head Relay privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Head Relay and the Head Relay Receiver have no accounts or analytics, and we run no server, so we
do not collect or store your personal data ourselves. The iPhone app shows an ad from a third party,
described below. The Windows receiver has no ads.

**Advertising.** The iPhone app shows one full-screen ad per launch, after your PC and AirPods
connect, through Google AdMob. Ads are not personalized and the app does not track you across other apps or websites. To serve and measure
the ad, Google may process data such as your IP address, device information, and ad interactions.
See https://policies.google.com/privacy. In regions that require it, the app asks for your consent
first.

**Head movement.** The iPhone app reads head orientation from your AirPods and sends it over your
local network to the PC you chose. It is used to move the view in your games and is not recorded.

**Finding your PC.** The iPhone app asks each address on your local network for a receiver. The
receiver answers with its computer name.

**PC sound.** If you turn this on in both apps, the receiver sends what your PC is playing to your
iPhone over your local network. It is played and not recorded. It is not encrypted, so use a
network you trust. It only goes to a phone paired with the receiver's QR code.

**Camera.** The iPhone app uses the camera only to read the QR code in the receiver's window. No
image is stored or sent.

**Stored on your devices.** The iPhone app keeps the PC address, the pairing key, and its settings.
The receiver keeps its settings in `%APPDATA%\HeadRelay\settings.json`. Removing the app or that file
removes them.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/cosmopolitanbeauty172-hub/head-relay-receiver/issues.
